From the Principal's Desk

Principal's Message

“Education is the most powerful weapon which you can use to change the world”
— Nelson Mandela

In a world that has witnessed rapid technological advancement, technical education serves as a vanguard to the future. The Institute of Printing Technology and Government Polytechnic College, as one of the oldest Institutions of technical education in the state, considers it as its responsibility to empower our learners to rise up to the challenges of the society and the industry. We draw strength from a glorious past that has enriched us with a host of prodigious alumni. The goodwill of the public and the support of the government is the wind beneath our wings that gives momentum to our journey towards excellence. We invite students to set in their foot into our campus as aspiring learners and depart as expert technicians of the highest calibre.
